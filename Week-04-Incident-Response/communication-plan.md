# Incident Communication Plan

## Vortex Tech Cybersecurity Internship 2026 — Week 4

### Incident

**Unauthorized Customer Database Access Through a Vulnerable API Endpoint**

---

## 1. Purpose

This communication plan defines who should be notified during the
hypothetical NovaCart Technologies security incident and when those
communications should take place.

The goal is to ensure that security, technical, leadership, legal,
and other relevant stakeholders receive accurate information at the
appropriate stage of the incident.

All communications should be based on verified information and
should avoid unnecessary speculation.

---

## 2. Communication Principles

During the incident, communications should be:

- Accurate
- Timely
- Relevant
- Clearly documented
- Shared only with appropriate stakeholders

Incident details should be handled carefully because the investigation
may initially contain incomplete information.

---

## 3. Internal Communication Timeline

| Approximate Time | Stakeholder | Reason for Notification | Information Shared |
|---|---|---|---|
| Immediately | SOC / Security Team | Begin incident triage | Initial alert, affected endpoint, observed activity |
| Immediately | IT / Engineering | Investigate affected service | API details, suspicious requests, technical indicators |
| Early Investigation | Security Leadership | Escalation and coordination | Initial scope, severity assessment, systems involved |
| Once Material Impact Is Suspected | Executive Leadership | Business-level awareness | Incident summary, potential customer impact, response status |
| During Investigation | Legal / Compliance | Assess obligations | Nature of exposed information, investigation status |
| During Response | Relevant System Owners | Coordinate remediation | Affected assets, required containment and recovery actions |

---

## 4. Security Team

### Timing

**Immediately after initial detection.**

### Responsibilities

The SOC/security team should:

- Validate the initial alert.
- Begin incident tracking.
- Preserve relevant evidence.
- Coordinate investigation activities.
- Maintain the incident timeline.
- Provide regular status updates.

### Initial Communication

The first notification should contain:

- Detection time
- Affected endpoint/system
- Initial observed activity
- Known or suspected impact
- Immediate actions taken
- Assigned incident owner

---

## 5. IT and Engineering

### Timing

**Immediately after the affected application or API is identified.**

### Responsibilities

IT and engineering teams should:

- Identify affected infrastructure.
- Assist with technical investigation.
- Apply approved containment measures.
- Support remediation.
- Validate system recovery.

### Information Shared

Technical teams should receive enough detail to act effectively,
including:

- Affected API endpoint
- Relevant request patterns
- Application/system information
- Security controls currently in place
- Required containment actions

---

## 6. Security Leadership

### Timing

**During the early investigation phase.**

Security leadership should receive an initial incident summary once
the security team has confirmed that the activity represents a
credible security incident.

### Information

The update should include:

- What happened
- When it was detected
- Systems affected
- Preliminary impact
- Investigation status
- Containment status
- Immediate risks
- Next response actions

---

## 7. Executive Leadership

### Timing

**Once material business or customer impact is suspected or confirmed.**

Executive leadership should receive a concise business-focused
summary rather than raw technical logs.

The summary should cover:

- Nature of the incident
- Potential business impact
- Potential customer impact
- Current response status
- Major decisions required
- Expected next steps

---

## 8. Legal and Compliance

### Timing

**During the investigation once potentially sensitive customer
information is identified.**

Legal and compliance teams should be involved to assess:

- The type of information potentially affected
- Applicable privacy obligations
- Notification requirements
- Contractual obligations
- Communication requirements

The technical team should provide verified facts to support the
assessment.

Exact notification requirements depend on the jurisdiction,
affected population, and applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

---

## 9. Customer Communication

### Timing

Customer notification should occur when the organization has
established enough verified information to determine that notification
is appropriate or legally required.

Potential communication may include:

- What happened
- What information may have been affected
- What the organization has done in response
- What customers should do, if applicable
- Where customers can obtain additional information

Public communication should be coordinated through the appropriate
leadership, legal, communications, and security teams.

---

## 10. Regulatory or External Notification

Where required by applicable data-protection laws, contractual
requirements, or other obligations, the appropriate authorities or
regulators should be notified within the required timeframe.

The decision and timing should be coordinated by the organization's
legal/compliance function using verified incident information.

---

## 11. Communication During Recovery

After containment and remediation, stakeholders should continue to
receive status updates covering:

- Vulnerability remediation
- Recovery progress
- Validation results
- Monitoring status
- Remaining risks

Communication should continue until the incident is formally closed.

---

## 12. Incident Closure Communication

At the end of the incident, the response team should provide a final
internal summary containing:

- Incident overview
- Root cause
- Confirmed impact
- Response actions
- Recovery status
- Lessons learned
- Preventive measures
- Outstanding follow-up actions

This final communication provides a common record for the
post-incident review.

---

## 13. Key Communication Rule

The organization should communicate **verified facts**, clearly
distinguishing confirmed findings from preliminary assumptions.

This helps prevent inaccurate information from spreading while the
investigation is still underway.
